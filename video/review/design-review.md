# Motion and sound review

The current trailer is an illustrated system of related parts. It supersedes
the screenshot cutouts and the 20-second reference-led study. Joe asked for
original motion and a richer composition after comparing those drafts with
[Boardwalk PR #24](https://github.com/jamditis/boardwalk-tutorials/pull/24).

The reference review examined its exact PR source, cue structure, musical
arrangement, and fifteen rendered frames in both formats. It established the
importance of connected transformations, distinct actions, musical development,
and a resolved ending. Its mascot, assets, music, source and scene concepts
were not copied into the Event Runner trailer.

Event Runner’s current visual idea is making room. Title, time, place and
speaker elements arrive on separate paths and join one session. Other sessions
form the program. A type/color choice propagates to the event heading, program
and speaker record; theme publication follows. Bookmarks fill, and copies of
the selected sessions travel into My schedule while the source program remains.
The result becomes a desktop event page and a phone schedule. Open arcs widen
around the product name and tagline. No UI capture is drawn in these trailers.

Landscape has a large decomposed opening and a separate type specimen during
the style transformation. Vertical moves bookmarked copies downward into two
personal agenda cards. All visible content comes from the appropriate project’s
semantic records. Public data is entirely fictional. Private content and media
remain outside the checkout and the public export allowlist.

The score is a new, original 112 BPM composition, 21 bars in 45 seconds. Its
modeled tine piano, synthesized percussion, filtered harmonic bass, detuned
ensemble, foley and diffused stereo room are all calculated in Node from
oscillators and seeded noise. No instrument bank, borrowed recording or loop
is used. Close extended voicings, syncopated comping, ghost notes, deliberate
breaks and a sustained F6/9 ending replace the retired repeating pluck.
The groove and previous voicing end at the pre-title breath; their releases and
delay taps cannot reappear after the new chord. Action cues share the picture’s
clock. Discrete bookmark and publication changes use the integer output frame
for every motion-blur sample.

The independent source review verified actual preset colors/fonts, speaker
relationships, and preservation of the public sessions when bookmarking.
It found a camera discontinuity and incomplete audio-dependency checks; both
were corrected and covered by regression cases. Audio source validation now
runs before rendering and final verification. Controlled broken relationship,
time, cue, dependency, privacy and geometry inputs are rejected.

The first full-size opening scan flagged rapidly moving high-contrast type.
The revised spatial treatment lowers contrast while parts travel, with a small
amount of lens softness, then restores sharp contrast at rest. The revised
185-frame camera/assembly sample passed the conservative native-pixel and
spatial-cohort flash screen with no hazardous pixel area. Full files have their
own reports; this internal screen is not a conformance certificate.

The review directory contains final-file format, AAC level/true-peak and flash
reports as they complete. PCM checks cover exact length, stereo/mono behavior,
clipping, DC offset, silent edges and the title pause. These measurements and
frame inspections do not constitute a headphone or laptop-speaker listening
session. A person must watch and listen to each complete cut before release.

Full-file frame review also found the outgoing reveal caption crossing the
closing tagline. The outgoing composition now clears before the title breath,
and the closing lockup begins on the resolving chord. Both 111-frame ending
partials passed native flash screening. Independent review confirmed the
separation, including shutter samples. Unchanged tutorial frames and the
trailer prefix before frame 1121 were reused; every changed ending frame was
rendered again from current source before full-file verification.

The final source review found one export freshness gap. The exporter now
rechecks score dependencies, the current flash analyzer, both movie hashes,
render inputs and flash pass status before copying any public file. A targeted
regression rejects five deliberately stale or failed verification records.
