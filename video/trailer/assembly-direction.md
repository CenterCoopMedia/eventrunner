# Event Runner: parts into an experience

Joe clarified the visual direction on October 11, 2026: deconstruct features,
functions and interactions, animate their constituent parts, then reassemble
them into an idealized experience. Screenshot cutouts are reference material,
not the visual construction of the trailer. This supersedes the literal-interface
construction of the 20-second study; its rhythmic build remains a useful reference.

The trailer is an illustrated product story, not a literal UI tutorial. Its
components are vector type, rules, surfaces, time rails, speaker records,
session titles and bookmark states. Each object keeps an identity through the
sequence. Moving a bookmark into My schedule leaves the original session in
the public program. Theme publication and content publication remain distinct.
Tutorials continue to show the actual interface and exact controls.

| Time | Transformation |
| --- | --- |
| 0–4 s | Separate title, time, room and speaker elements enter along a ruled edge. Their arrangement hints at a session before a containing surface exists. The camera makes room for the assembled program. |
| 4–13 s · Plan | The parts separate into an exploded view, then travel on separate curved paths into one session. Time rails and other sessions grow around it to form the program. The speaker record connects to its session. |
| 13–22 s · Shape | The event's heading, program, and speaker elements separate. One style change travels across them, changing type and accent together. The theme is published; the parts rejoin. |
| 22–33 s · Gather | Bookmarks fill on selected sessions. Copies of those sessions move into My schedule while the public program remains intact. The attendee's day becomes visible. |
| 33–39 s | Page identity, program, speakers and personal schedule assemble into a coherent desktop-and-phone event experience. The relationships draw the pieces together. Open arcs widen to make room for the title. |
| 39–45 s | Event Runner and Make room for your event. arrive with the resolving chord. The official mark remains unchanged. Everything settles with at least two seconds remaining. |
