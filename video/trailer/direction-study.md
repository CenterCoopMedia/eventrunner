# Replacement trailer direction

The first trailer treatment was rejected by Joe on October 11, 2026: its
picture, sound and feel did not meet the platform-trailer brief or the
Boardwalk/NJ PBS reference. Its previous quality-review notes describe a
superseded treatment, not an accepted result. Final delivery renders are paused.

This 20-second study tests the replacement direction before the longer public
and client trailers inherit it. It uses the existing fictional UI captures.
Study media and the provisional clock live in external review storage and are
not included in the public exporter.

| Seconds | Picture and sound |
| --- | --- |
| 0–3 | A ruled edge opens into a blue prototype surface derived from the real homepage hero. The camera pulls back through that same measured layout; the actual interface appears after the camera lands. Tuned arrivals introduce the score. |
| 3–6 | A blue plane takes the frame. Large Plan types; its caret leads to the real headline field. Captured typing carries the action as the bass and percussion enter. |
| 6–11.5 | A frame draws Shape. The real Site style control changes to Newsroom and stays visible while the real phone preview appears alongside/below it. Its separate Publish the theme action is shown before leaving the style workflow. The arrangement briefly thins, then builds. |
| 11.5–13.5 | Gather joins from opposing sides. A real Save and publish press and its captured confirmation carry the content-publishing action. |
| 13.5–17 | The changed hero returns as a blue prototype surface and pulls back into the same page geometry as the opening. The actual, signed-out public page appears after the camera lands and holds before folding into the ruled edge. A short musical breath precedes the name. |
| 17–20 | Event Runner rises with the resolving chord. The unchanged official mark and Make room for your event. settle by 18 seconds and hold through the end. |

The reference is [Boardwalk PR #24](https://github.com/jamditis/boardwalk-tutorials/pull/24),
head `5147d7ee0fe4daf650d5d1c4c47db069f3ee1022`. Its Front page composition,
clock, score structure and locally rendered frames were inspected. The older
public teaser player did not yet expose this composition during review. No
Boardwalk assets, recordings, or scene source are bundled in Event Runner.

The new score is an arrangement, with a sparse opening, a bass-and-percussion
drop, transition fills, moving air, tuned landings and a resolving ending.
It is synthesized from oscillators and seeded noise. Tutorials keep their
separate quiet score and flat presentation.

The reference principle is continuity: each action motivates the next visual
and musical event. Typography owns the frame; screens change size and position
with the story. The earlier side-by-side heading/screenshot template is retired.

Before presentation: inspect both formats, check native-resolution flash
analysis, measure AAC loudness and true peak, and verify cue timing. A full
human watch/listen remains necessary; signal checks cannot judge the feeling.
