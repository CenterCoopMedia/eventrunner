### Family 6: the option groups of §4

Every style carries five new groups, each remapping declared tokens only
(`design/tokens/presets/*.json`; the catalog mirrors regenerated):

| Group | Civic | Newsroom | Broadsheet | Atlas | Field Guide | Zine |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Long read opening | plain | standfirst | drop cap | plain | drop cap | plain |
| Quote device | ruled minute, plain rules | ruled with mark, side rule, mark only | centred with mark, column rule | route line, boxed sign | field note, pressed page | handwritten, toner block, cut-out |
| Directory style | ruled entries, portrait plates | portrait shelf, tall portraits | agate directory, plate portraits | gazetteer, station index | specimen plates, field list | contact sheet, cut-outs |
| Section boundary | three | three | three | two (no margin folio) | three | two (no margin folio) |
| Table rules | full grid | ruled head | full grid (hairline head) | full grid | ruled head (hairline) | ruled head |

The defaults follow the record where it names one (Broadsheet and Field
Guide open on the cap, Zine and Atlas plain) and the style's own story where
it does not. The section boundary's "folio in the margin" knocks the folio
out of the rule where the margin column opens beside the measure; Zine's
struck folios and Atlas's grid have no room for it, so those two offer the
first two choices only. Two contracts were added so an option had tokens to
move: `directory` (the shelf's rule and portrait, the index's row) and two
folio tokens on `section-rule`. `resolveComponentFonts` now reads a picked
choice's `componentFonts`, so Zine's toner-block quote can drop the script
face; the catalog builder keeps that key on a choice.

### Family 7: the tabs and the segmented control take an unavailable state

Decision: both take `disabled` per item, drawn as `aria-disabled="true"`, not
`disabled`. The item stays in the row and in the arrow-key ring so a reader
can land on it and hear why (`hint` is read as part of its name); its handler
refuses every activation path (click, Enter, Space, and arrow selection), the
arrow keys move on from the focused item rather than from the selected one,
and both test files prove the refusal. The specimen book draws the state on
both controls. The speaker session hub (issue 212) can therefore show a tab
for a panel that exists and cannot be opened yet; a tab with nothing behind
it at all is still not rendered.

## Decisions the owner may want to change

- The `when` card is a fact too, not a stat. A date range is a term and a
  description; the previous stat asked for a source line for the dates. The
  demo's evidence-bearing `info__when` stat is replaced by the fact.
- A second block type, `quote`, landed with the pull quote so the device has
  a content slot. Its fields are `text` and an optional `attribution`.
- `SessionState` no longer draws through `Tag`. The record asks for a word with
  an ink change and a rule, and a finished session in the back-issue ink; the
  tag's boxed ground could not carry the ink change.
- `ProfilePhoto` lost its `rounded-brand` utility: the avatar's corner is the
  `--avatar-radius` token so a style can move it (Zine, Broadsheet and Field
  Guide square it). The default is still the brand radius.
- The preset catalog mirrors (`presetCatalog.cjs`, `presetCopy.js`,
  `presets/README.md`) are committed with the source change, not in the
  regenerate commit: they are not on the director's regenerate list and their
  freshness test fails without them.

## The bundle budget

`scripts/ci/bundle-budget.cjs` passes on the client build
(`apps/web/dist`: 1,077,017 raw bytes and 287,398 gzip bytes against
1,080,000 and 292,000). On the demo build (`docs/demo`) it fails on the raw
figure only: 1,087,622 raw bytes, 7,622 over, with 291,409 gzip inside the
budget. The base commit's demo measured 1,068,974 raw; the wave adds about
18.6KB, and most of it is the preset catalog, which grew from 34.7KB to
60.2KB of source with the five option groups and the remaps every device
needs. What could be cut was cut: every choice carries only the tokens it
moves, the drop cap contract lost four tokens to a style query, and the
content page reads the stored template id instead of importing the template
table. Raising the budget is the owner's call, so the budget file is
untouched. If the wave is accepted as designed, the raw initial budget needs
about 1,100,000 (the gzip margin is 600 bytes, so 300,000 there would give
the stack room). The demo tier's CI job will fail on this until then.
