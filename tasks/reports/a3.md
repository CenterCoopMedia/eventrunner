### Family 1: the definition list, the fact block (#234), and the pull quote

- `design/tokens/components.json`: `definition-list` and `pull-quote`
  contracts. Every preset remaps both (`design/tokens/presets/*.json`), and
  the catalog mirrors were regenerated (`packages/shared/src/presetCatalog.cjs`,
  `design/tokens/presets/README.md`).

- `apps/web/src/components/editorial/DefinitionList.jsx` (+ `DefinitionPair`),
  `PullQuote.jsx`, and tests that render both in every style and both modes

  the registry on both sides (`functions/src/cms/blockTypes.cjs`,
  `apps/web/src/admin/blockTypes.js`), the renderer registry, `SectionBlocks`

- `scripts/lib/seed.cjs`: the key facts section seeds three facts (when, where,
  who); when and where come from `config/event`; `info__where_venue` and
  `info__where_address` are obsolete ids; the details section admits a quote.
  `scripts/lib/demo-event.cjs`: the fixture carries the three facts and one

- `docs/ADMIN_GUIDE.md`, `docs/design-reference.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`.
- Specimen book: pull quote, definition list, and fact block figures in the
  editorial section.

"Done when" for #234: an operator can publish a where or who card without
inventing evidence fields (the fact block has three fields and no evidence

`functions/src/cms/blockTypes.test.cjs`); tests cover both block shapes

## Decisions the owner may want to change

- The `when` card is a fact too, not a stat. A date range is a term and a
  description; the previous stat asked for a source line for the dates. The
  demo's evidence-bearing `info__when` stat is replaced by the fact.
- A second block type, `quote`, landed with the pull quote so the device has
  a content slot. Its fields are `text` and an optional `attribution`.
- The preset catalog mirrors (`presetCatalog.cjs`, `presetCopy.js`,
  `presets/README.md`) are committed with the source change, not in the
  regenerate commit: they are not on the director's regenerate list and their
  freshness test fails without them.
