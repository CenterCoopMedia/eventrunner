Event sites from the Center for Cooperative Media.

Event Runner gives nonprofits, newsrooms, universities, and event producers a website their own staff can manage. The Center for Cooperative Media at Montclair State University operates the service.

Plan your event site Visit the Center

Made for the work around the event.

A conference website keeps changing. Speakers send new biographies. Sessions move rooms. Attendees need the latest schedule. Event Runner puts those updates in the hands of the people running the event.

The project grew from the Collaborative Journalism Summit website. Its schedule, speaker, registration, and publishing tools now form a separate platform for other events.

Your event and your content.

Your organization keeps ownership of what it publishes. Each event has its own site and data. Your staff work in the admin panel; the Event Runner team manages the hosting.

The service includes setup, onboarding, and support. Your agreement describes the service and its fees.

Open source.
Open to contributions.

The code is available under the Apache-2.0 license. Contributors can inspect it, report problems, and propose improvements. The Event Runner name remains reserved to the Center for Cooperative Media.

Engineering documentation lives with the code: architecture decisions, deployment runbooks, and contribution instructions are available in GitHub.
