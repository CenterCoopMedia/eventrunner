A program people can use.

Every session gets a time, a room, a page, and its speakers. Attendees search the program and build their own schedule.

  • Organize sessions by day and track.
  • Collect proposals and let speakers complete their own profiles.
  • Save bookmarks, private notes, and calendar files.

Make the change. Then publish it.

Edit pages, add images, and send updates without changing code. Work stays a draft until you publish it.

  • Build pages from text, images, links, and other blocks.
  • Post event updates and time-limited announcements.
  • Email attendees or speakers after a preview and a test send.
Watch a page update →

Tickets on your terms.

Use your own Stripe or Eventbrite account, import a ticket list, or run an event without tickets. Ticket money and refunds stay with your organization.

  • Collect registration answers for each attendee.
  • Manage approvals and event check-in.
  • Keep dietary and access information out of public profiles.
