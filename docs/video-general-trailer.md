# General trailer: Make room for your event

The current 45-second trailer follows Joe’s October 11 direction to deconstruct
features and interactions, then reassemble them into a coherent event experience.
It replaces the screenshot treatment and the Boardwalk-inspired study.
Apply the shared [production plan](video-production.md).

The public content is the fictional Harborlight Forum, read directly from the
same fixture as the tutorials. The trailer illustrates capabilities rather than
teaching exact clicks. Tutorials show the literal interface.

| Capability | Source and limit |
| --- | --- |
| Sessions connect titles, times, rooms and speakers into a program | [Staff handbook](handbook/for-event-staff.md); IDs and relationships come from the fixture. |
| A theme changes shared type and accent | [Branding guide](ADMIN_GUIDE.md#settings--branding); exact Institutional and Newsroom preset values are read from product source. Theme publication is a separate action. |

| “Plan. Shape. Gather.” / “Make room for your event.” | Joe’s approved phrases; editorial copy, not measured outcomes. |

No client names, faces, registrations, customer marks, performance claims,
attendance promises or endorsements enter the public set.

## Music and review

The original composition uses electric piano, synthesized percussion, modeled bass,
a detuned harmonic ensemble and a diffused stereo room. Extended chord voicings,
syncopated comping, ghost notes and deliberate breaks give it movement and space.
Every sound is synthesized from oscillators and seeded noise in Node. There is
no instrument bank, borrowed reference track, music loop, copied melody or narration.

The master targets −16 LUFS, and encoded AAC must remain below −1 dBTP.
Source checks cover every composition dependency and synthesis sources.
Flash analysis, every-ninth-frame sheets, full-size critical-frame inspection,
and an independent correctness review accompany the outputs. A person must
still watch and listen on headphones and laptop speakers before release.

Outputs: `general-trailer-landscape.mp4` and `general-trailer-vertical.mp4`.
The reproducible commands and remaining delivery gates are in
[video/README.md](../video/README.md).
