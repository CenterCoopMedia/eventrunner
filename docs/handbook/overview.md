# Meet Event Runner

Event Runner gives your conference or gathering a website your team can keep up to date. Bring the schedule, speakers, tickets, practical information, and event updates together in one place. The Center for Cooperative Media sets up and hosts the site; your team manages the content.

## Find your starting point

- **Planning an event?** Read [For event organizers](for-clients.md) to see what is included and what you need to get started.
- **Working on an event site?** Start with [For event staff](for-event-staff.md), then use the [Admin guide](../ADMIN_GUIDE.md) for a particular task.
- **Going to an event?** [For attendees](for-attendees.md) explains sign-in, saved sessions, materials, and your profile.

## What your site can do

Publish sessions, speaker profiles, pages, and updates from the admin panel. Keep drafts separate from published content. Give attendees a schedule they can browse, save, and add to their calendar. Connect the registration option agreed for your event, including Eventbrite, Stripe, or a spreadsheet.

Your site uses your event's identity. Choose a site style, add your logos, and set your colors. Your team edits content without changing code. Available features and staff permissions depend on how your event is configured.

## Explore before you start

The [static visitor preview](https://centercoopmedia.github.io/eventrunner/demo/) shows a selected historical program from an NC Local event, with eight named sessions and 25 public speakers; it has no admin panel, and sign-in and registration are disabled. The separate [hosted admin/client demo](https://eventrunner-demo.web.app/) includes an [admin panel](https://eventrunner-demo.web.app/admin), and admin access requires an authorized account.

## Get help with your event

Ask your event team about tickets, accessibility needs, or changes to your registration. For help running a site or starting a new one, see [Getting help](getting-help.md). The [frequently asked questions](faq.md) cover common questions about the service.
