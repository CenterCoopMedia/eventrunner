A few things
to know.

More questions answered →
Does our team need a developer?

No. Your team edits pages, speakers, and sessions in the admin panel. Event Runner handles the site setup and hosting.

Can we use our own ticketing account?

Yes. Event Runner supports organizer-owned Eventbrite and Stripe accounts, plus attendee imports. We’ll confirm the setup that fits your event.

Who owns the event data?

Your organization owns its event content and data. Event Runner provides export tools and an archive handoff process. Read the organizer guide.

What can we try in the demo?

The demo shows a historical NC Local event. Explore the program, speakers, and site styles without creating an account. It does not accept real registrations or send email.
