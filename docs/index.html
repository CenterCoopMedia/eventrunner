Conference websites your team can run

Event Runner builds and hosts a website for your event, with a schedule, speaker pages and ticket links. Your team keeps it up to date without a developer, and you get direct help from the person who runs the service.

See a demo Get in touch

How it works

  1. Get your signup link
  2. Answer a few questions and accept the agreement
  3. We build your site
  4. Your team runs it from the admin panel

What each site includes

Schedule and speakers

Sessions, bookmarks and calendar links, with speaker pages your team can review and publish.

Pages your team edits

Draft and publish pages from an admin panel, with no code and no waiting on a developer.

Tickets your way

Connect your own Eventbrite or Stripe account, or keep attendees in a spreadsheet. Ticket money goes to your account.

Help from a person

The Event Runner team personally supports every client, from your first question through your event. Email info@eventrunner.org.
