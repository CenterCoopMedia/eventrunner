### Changed

- Busy buttons in the admin and on the public site name the work they are
  doing ("Saving the session…", "Sending the invoice…") instead of a bare
  "Saving…", "Loading…" or "Sending…". Past ten seconds the words say the
  work is still going, and a screen reader hears that once. Busy buttons keep
  keyboard focus. The access list's Try again offers a fresh read when a
  retry has not answered in ten seconds (#618).
