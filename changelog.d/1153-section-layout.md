### Added

- Each page section can choose its layout by picture: Reading width (the
  default and today's look), Wide, or Two columns. Two columns put the first
  half of the section's blocks on the left and the rest on the right, and
  fold to one column on a phone. Beside the choice, the page editor draws the
  section's own content in the site's theme at a computer width and a phone
  width. A section that never chooses keeps its look and stores nothing new.
