### Added

- Organizers can offer optional add-ons with chosen ticket types, such as a reception with its
  own price, description, capacity and a required acknowledgement like "I am 21 or older". The
  server prices each add-on; a free ticket with a paid add-on charges only the add-on. Orders,
  confirmation emails, check-in, the organizer ticket views and the exports list add-ons, and an
  organizer can refund or cancel an add-on while the ticket keeps admission (#1412).
